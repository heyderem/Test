## Style rules seen in both videos

- **Arms flare.** Arms rarely hang straight; even idle has the arms 5-10 degrees out. In the
  sprint the arms swing hard forward/back *and* out to the sides (back arm 25-35 degrees out).
- **The torso leads, the head stays level.** Torso leans and twists; the head counter-rotates to
  keep looking forward and stays near horizontal (camera-friendly).
- **Shoulder twist in cycles.** Walk and sprint twist the torso toward the forward leg's opposite
  arm (counter-rotation), clearly visible from behind.
- **Snappy contacts, soft overs.** Contacts and hits arrive fast (ease-out), poses then drift.
- **Wide, readable silhouettes from behind.** Most poses are designed to read from a chase
  camera: limbs spread to the sides rather than hidden in front of/behind the body.
- **Legs reach.** Strides are long (thigh swing 45-55 degrees in the sprint), feet never sink.
- **Overlap.** Arms and head trail the torso by 1-3 frames and settle after stops.
